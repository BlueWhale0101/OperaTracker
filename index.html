Opera Tracker

Track operas you've attended or listened to, books you've read, notes, ratings, and your progress across the canon.
Your data saves automatically in this browser with local storage. Use Export Data to move it between devices, and Import Data to restore it elsewhere.

How to use this tracker

Mark an opera as Seen Live, Listened, or both. Add a rating, the production or recording you used, and your notes. For books, mark Planned, Reading, or Read and add notes or takeaways.

You can also add your own books and operas that are not in the starter library. Custom items export and import with the rest of your data.

Single-file app. Save this as index.html and deploy anywhere static, including GitHub Pages.
